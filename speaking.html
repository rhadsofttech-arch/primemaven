Short bio

Adewunmi Erijide Adeniji (Primemaven) is a Lagos-based full stack developer, TEDx speaker and the CEO/Founder of MyPropifyNG, a real estate platform that protects Nigerians from property fraud through verified listings. He also founded Rhadsoft Tech, a software studio that has delivered 50+ projects for 30+ clients.

Long bio

Adewunmi Erijide Adeniji, known as Primemaven, is a full stack developer, founder and TEDx speaker based in Lagos, Nigeria. He builds software that fits how Nigerian businesses actually work.

He is the CEO and Founder of MyPropifyNG, a real estate platform launched in 2025 across Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt. MyPropifyNG puts every listing through a 3-stage verification process of document authentication, physical inspection and partner validation, helping renters, buyers and students find homes without the risk of fraud.

In 2021 he founded Rhadsoft Tech, a software studio with bases in Surulere and Ikorodu that designs and builds web platforms, mobile apps, ERP systems and AI solutions. Rhadsoft has delivered more than 50 projects for over 30 clients and runs its own products, including MyPropifyNG, BookoraNG, IkoroduSquare and BookMeNaija.

Adewunmi also leads the Ikorodu chapter of Zev Builders, the developer community of Zevop, where he helps local developers learn, build and ship. He speaks and moderates at technology and industry events on software, entrepreneurship and building tech in Nigeria.
