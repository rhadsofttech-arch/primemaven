About me

A developer who understands how businesses actually run.

I'm Adewunmi Erijide Adeniji, known online as Primemaven. I'm a full stack developer based in Lagos, and I enjoy turning messy, manual processes into simple software people want to use.

I'm the CEO and Founder of MyPropifyNG, a real estate platform that helps Nigerians find homes through verified listings, without the risk of fraud. I also run Rhadsoft Tech, my software company, where I build my own products and custom software for clients.

I'm also a TEDx speaker, and I speak at and moderate sessions at tech events. As the software developer at IEMA Standards Limited, an ISO certification and training company, I build the internal systems the business runs on.
